Admins get their own area at `app.dataleap.ai/admin`. The **Management** section covers who's in the workspace and what applies to everyone.

## Members & roles

Invite people, assign roles, and manage access:

- **Roles:** every member is an **Admin** or a **Member** — switchable per person.
- **Team admins:** members can additionally administer specific teams (shown per user). Whoever creates a team becomes its admin.
- **Invite Members** handles new joiners. Access requests and invites that haven't been accepted yet have their own tabs: **Pending requests** and **Pending invites**.

### Follow up on a pending invite

The **Pending invites** tab lists everyone who has been invited but hasn't joined yet, with their role and when the invite was last sent. Each invite shows a status:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | The invitation still works. The person hasn't accepted it yet. |
| **Expired** | The invitation ran out before the person accepted it. |
| **Needs recovery** | The invitation can no longer be used. |

Hover over an invite to see its actions:

- **Send again** sends the invitation again. For an **Expired** or **Needs recovery** invite, you confirm first and the person gets a new invitation. Their role stays the same.
- **Cancel** withdraws the invite.

You can also invite the same email again with **Invite Members**. If the earlier invitation has expired or can no longer be used, Dataleap sends a new one. You only get an error if the person has already joined, or if their invite is still **Pending** — use **Send again** for that.

## Teams

See every [team](/share/team-spaces) in your organization, its members, and when it was created. Any member can create a team, so you don't have to set them up for people. They can also ask to join a team, and its team admins approve.

As an organization admin, you manage every team here, including teams you're not in, and you can add yourself to one. A team's agents, templates, and skills are for its members, so you only see them once you're in the team.

<Note>
  On Enterprise plans, a new team may wait for an organization admin to approve its budget before its agents can run. See [Credits & usage](/admin/credits-and-usage).
</Note>

## Organization defaults

The **Organization** page sets what applies to every agent in the workspace:

- **Workspace defaults** — display currency and the timezone used for analytics and team runs.
- **System prompt** — organization-level instructions **applied to every agent in the organization** (up to 12,000 characters). The place for company-wide rules: working language, PII handling, tone. Builders never have to remember them; every agent gets them automatically.
