Admins set this on the **Models** page under **Controls** at `app.dataleap.ai/admin`. Only **Admins** can open it. Members pick from what you allow.

The page has two jobs: what **Basic**, **Pro**, and **Expert** mean in your workspace, and which models members can choose.

## Presets

Most people pick a preset, not a specific model.

| Preset | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Basic** | Quick tasks and high volume |
| **Pro** | Balanced performance — the default for running agents |
| **Expert** | Complex, multi-step reasoning |

You choose which model and **Reasoning Level** power each preset. The available levels depend on the model. When a model supports more than one, choose from **Minimal**, **Low**, **Medium**, **High**, **Extra high**, or **Max**. A **Customized** badge appears when a preset no longer uses Dataleap's default. **Reset** puts it back.

<Frame caption="Pick the model and Reasoning Level behind a preset.">
  <img src="/images/admin-models-presets.png" alt="A preset's model picker open, listing available models by provider with a Reasoning Level slider" />
</Frame>

A preset change applies to agents created after you save it. An existing agent stays on its preset and keeps the model and **Reasoning Level** it had when it was created.

Picking a different preset on an existing agent gives you that preset as it was when the agent was created, not the model you just saved. To move an existing agent to another model, open its model selector, choose **Customize**, and pick the model.

You can't turn off a model that currently powers a preset. Change the preset first.

## Model access

**Model Access** is the list of models members can use. Turn a model on or off with its switch.

Enabled models appear when someone opens **Customize** in the model selector. Disabled models do not.

If you disable a model agents already use, their future runs may be blocked until someone changes that agent's model.

Save from the bar at the bottom. Dataleap asks you to review first whenever you change a preset, or turn off a model that agents already use.

## When Dataleap retires a model

Sometimes Dataleap stops supporting a model, for example when its provider stops serving it. Agents on that model don't break. On a set switch date, Dataleap moves them to a successor model for you.

### Before the switch date

- The model shows a **Retiring** badge in **Model Access**. Hover it to see the switch date and the successor.
- Nobody can newly pick the model. It disappears from **Customize** for new choices, you can't turn it on if it's off, and you can't choose it for a preset.
- Agents already on the model keep running on it until the switch.
- The owners of affected agents and your organization's admins get an email with the switch date and the successor. There's nothing they need to do.

### On the switch date

- Every agent and sub-agent on the model moves to the successor. An agent on a preset stays on that preset.
- Each one keeps its **Reasoning Level** if the successor has it. If not, it runs at the nearest level the successor supports.
- Presets that used the model move to the successor too.
- In **Model Access**, the successor is turned on and the retiring model is turned off.

<Note>
  If a provider stops serving a model without warning, Dataleap may switch agents right away and send the email afterwards.
</Note>

### Choose a different successor

When a retirement is scheduled, a **Scheduled retirements** section appears between the presets and **Model Access**. Each row shows the retiring model, its switch date, and how many agents use it.

<Frame caption="Scheduled retirements: upcoming switches with a Successor menu, and switches that already happened.">
  <img src="/images/admin-models-retirements.svg" alt="The Scheduled retirements section on the Models page. Two rows have already switched and show Moved to with the successor. One upcoming row shows its switch date, the number of agents, and a Successor menu marked Recommended by Dataleap." />
</Frame>

The **Successor** menu starts on Dataleap's choice, marked **Recommended**. To move your organization's agents somewhere else, pick another model. Your choice saves right away and isn't part of **Save changes**. **Reset** goes back to Dataleap's recommendation.

You can change the successor until the switch date. After the switch, the row shows where agents moved. Earlier retirements are listed under **Past retirements**.

## What members see

In chat, **Workflow**, and a sub-agent's **Tools** tab, people pick **Basic**, **Pro**, or **Expert** — or **Customize** to choose a specific enabled model and its **Reasoning Level**.

<Frame caption="The model selector members see in chat.">
  <img src="/images/admin-models-member-selector.png" alt="The member model selector showing Basic, Pro, Expert, and a Customize option" />
</Frame>

Which models appear under **Customize** is the list you enable here. Dataleap does not publish a fixed catalog in these docs; the list changes as models are added or [retired](#when-dataleap-retires-a-model).

Setup conversations can't use the **Basic** preset. Building stays on **Pro** or **Expert**, or on a custom model.

Some presets depend on your plan. **Expert** needs a paid plan. Models you enable under **Customize** are available to members on every plan.

When to raise or lower a preset is covered on [Sub-agents & model tiers](/scale/sub-agents-and-intelligence-levels#model-tiers).
