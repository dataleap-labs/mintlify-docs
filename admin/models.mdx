Admins set this on the **Models** page under **Controls** at `app.dataleap.ai/admin`. Only **Admins** can open it. Members pick from what you allow.

The page has two jobs: what **Basic**, **Pro**, and **Expert** mean in your workspace, and which models members can choose.

## Presets

Most people pick a preset, not a specific model.

| Preset | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Basic** | Quick tasks and high volume |
| **Pro** | Balanced performance — the default for running agents |
| **Expert** | Complex, multi-step reasoning |

You choose which model and **Reasoning Level** power each preset. The available levels depend on the model. When a model supports more than one, choose from **Minimal**, **Low**, **Medium**, **High**, **Extra high**, or **Max**. A **Customized** badge appears when a preset no longer uses Dataleap's default. **Reset** puts it back.

<Frame caption="Pick the model and Reasoning Level behind a preset.">
  <img src="/images/admin-models-presets.png" alt="A preset's model picker open, listing available models by provider with a Reasoning Level slider" />
</Frame>

Preset changes apply to every member and agent using them. Agents keep their preset and switch models on their next new activity.

You can't turn off a model that currently powers a preset. Change the preset first.

## Model access

**Model Access** is the list of models members can use. Turn a model on or off with its switch.

Enabled models appear when someone opens **Customize** in the model selector. Disabled models do not.

If you disable a model agents already use, their future runs may be blocked until someone changes that agent's model.

Save from the bar at the bottom. If the change affects agents, Dataleap asks you to review first.

## What members see

In chat, **Workflow**, and a sub-agent's **Tools** tab, people pick **Basic**, **Pro**, or **Expert** — or **Customize** to choose a specific enabled model and its **Reasoning Level**.

<Frame caption="The model selector members see in chat.">
  <img src="/images/admin-models-member-selector.png" alt="The member model selector showing Basic, Pro, Expert, and a Customize option" />
</Frame>

Which models appear under **Customize** is the list you enable here. Dataleap does not publish a fixed catalog in these docs; the list changes as models are added or retired.

Setup conversations can't use the **Basic** preset. Building stays on **Pro** or **Expert**, or on a custom model.

Some presets depend on your plan. **Expert** needs a paid plan. Models you enable under **Customize** are available to members on every plan.

When to raise or lower a preset is covered on [Sub-agents & model tiers](/scale/sub-agents-and-intelligence-levels#model-tiers).
