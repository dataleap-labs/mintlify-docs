---
title: "When to share what"
description: "A simple decision guide: personal agent, team agent, sharing an agent, template, locked template — or a skill."
---

Value compounds when agents stop being personal and start being shared. Use this table to pick the right way:

| Choose | When | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 🧍 **Personal agent** | You're experimenting, the workflow only serves you, or it works with personal data (your inbox, your notes). | Stays private in your [personal space](/share/team-spaces). |
| 👥 **Team agent** | The work is the team's — the team should own it and keep it running, even when someone leaves. | Lives in a [team space](/share/team-spaces), on the team's budget. Team members can edit it by default. It uses the team's Shared accounts, and someone's Personal account only with their approval. |
| 💬 **Share the agent** | Others should chat with this agent, or help you build it, while it stays where it is. | Add people or teams from **Share** with **Can use**, **Can edit**, or **Can manage**. See [Share an agent](/share/use-only). |
| 📋 **Template** | Others should clone a proven workflow and adapt it to their context. | [Open template](/share/templates) — fully editable clones. |
| 🔒 **Locked template** | Many people must run the **identical** process. | [Locked template](/share/locked-templates) — owner updates reach every clone. |
| 🖥️ **App** | People should use a tool built on your agent — see current data, fill in a form, press a button — without chatting with the agent. | [App](/scale/apps) — share the app itself. Everyone who opens it acts with the agent's integrations. |
| 📈 **Dashboard** | You want people to see a *result*, not run the agent that made it. | [Dashboard](/scale/dashboards) — share the page itself, view only. |
| 🧩 **Skill** | A standard or definition that 2+ agents should follow. | [Skill](/scale/skills) — write it once; personal, team, or org-wide. |
