Leaving the company, ending a contract, or moving to another role? You can't pass a personal agent on to someone else as it is. Instead, decide for each agent where it belongs, and move the ones other people rely on before your access ends.

## What happens when you leave

Once you're removed from your organization in Dataleap:

| | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Your personal agents** | They're turned off, so they stop running. |
| **Team agents** | They stay with the team and keep running. |
| **Integrations connected with your own account** | They leave every team with you. Team agents that used them lose those tools and triggers. |
| **Skills you own** | They're deleted, including skills you shared with a team. |

So the agent itself is rarely the problem. What breaks is anything still tied to you: your personal space, your logins, and your skills.

## Decide where each agent belongs

| The agent | Do this |
|---|---|
| Only helps your own productivity, such as your inbox or your notes | Nothing. It's tied to you, and nobody else needs it. |
| Would help colleagues with their own work | Share it as a [template](/share/templates). Each colleague deploys their own copy and connects their own integrations. |
| Does work for your team, or others rely on its results | Copy it to a [team space](/share/team-spaces). The team owns the copy, so it keeps running after you leave. |

Work that belongs to a team should live in a team space anyway, not only when someone leaves. [When to share what](/share/when-to-share-what) has the full decision guide.

## Move a team workflow to a team space

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick or create the team">

The copy goes into a team you're a member of. Your teams are listed under **Teams** in the sidebar, and **All teams** shows every team in your organization.

If no team fits, create one: click **+** next to **Teams**, enter a name, and add your successor, for example your manager, under **Add Members**. You become the new team's admin.

</Step>
<Step title="Copy the agent to the team">

Open the agent, click its name at the top of the page, and choose **Copy to team**. Pick the team under **Select team** and confirm with **Copy to team**.

The team gets its own copy. Your original stays unchanged in your personal space.

</Step>
<Step title="Set up the copy with team accounts">

The copy includes no credentials, and it starts turned off. Dataleap opens a setup chat in the copy that walks you through connecting integrations and setting up triggers for the team.

Have your successor connect the integrations with their own login or a [service account](/admin/connected-apps-management), not yours. Anything connected with your account stops working for the team when you leave.

</Step>
<Step title="Make your successor a team admin">

Open the team, click **Settings**, and under **Members** open the **⋯** menu next to your successor's name. Choose **Make team admin**.

Team admins can manage every agent in the team and add new members. A team's only admin can't leave it.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  On Enterprise plans, an organization admin may need to approve a new team's budget before its agents can run.
</Note>

### What stays behind

The copy starts fresh in a few ways:

- Past runs and chats stay with your original agent.
- The copy's [database](/build/agent-database) starts empty.
- An [app](/scale/apps) built on the original doesn't come along. Ask the agent in the copy to build it again.

## Hand over your skills

Skills you own are deleted when you leave, even if a team agent uses them. Before you go, [export](/scale/skills#import-and-export-skills) each skill others need. A teammate imports it, shares it with the team, and asks the team's agents to use the new copy.
