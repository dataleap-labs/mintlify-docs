Ask your agent for a dashboard and it builds you a page: the numbers, charts, and tables you asked for, in a browser tab you can send to someone else. The same request also produces reports, maps, and calculators — underneath they are all the same thing, a single page your agent writes for you.

You don't switch this on. When your request is better answered by a page than by a chat message, your agent builds one.

## Before you start

Your agent can only chart data it can reach. Give it a source first: a [connected app](/build/connect-your-tools), [knowledge](/build/add-knowledge) you uploaded, or figures you paste straight into the chat.

## Ask for one

Describe the result and who it is for. Your agent chooses the layout.

- "Build me a dashboard of this quarter's pipeline, broken down by owner."
- "Turn last month's support tickets into a one-page report for the team meeting."
- "Put our office locations on a map I can send to a new starter."

If the first version isn't right, say so in the chat. Ask for a different breakdown, fewer charts, or a shorter summary, and your agent builds it again.

## What a dashboard can do

A dashboard is a real web page, not a picture of one. That gives you more than a static export.

| Ask for | What you get |
|---|---|
| Headline numbers | A summary across the top: the figures that matter, each with the comparison or context that makes it mean something |
| Charts | Bar, line, area, and doughnut charts for a straightforward breakdown, and richer mixed charts when one page needs several views of the same data |
| Maps | An interactive map you can pan and zoom, with your locations marked on it |
| Diagrams | Flows, org charts, and process maps |
| Detail tables | Grouped tables with units and aligned numbers, so a column of figures stays readable |
| Calculators | Inputs someone can change to see the effect, worked out as they type |

### Things people can do on the page

The page carries its own code, so it doesn't have to sit still. Ask for filters, a toggle between two views, or a search box over a long table, and your agent can build them in. Because all of it runs in the reader's browser, those controls keep working for everyone you send the page to — no account, no set-up on their side.

### Showing it to people

Dashboards are built to be shown. They come out looking like a polished report rather than an app screen: a clear title, a short line of context, the headline figures, then the detail. They reflow to fit a phone as well as a laptop, and a report-style page prints cleanly if someone wants it on paper.

## What it can't do

<Note>
  **A dashboard is a snapshot.** It holds the data your agent had at the moment it built the page. It doesn't refresh on its own, and it can't reach your connected apps while you're looking at it. For current figures, ask your agent again — or put the request on a [trigger](/build/triggers) so a fresh dashboard arrives on a schedule.
</Note>

Two more limits worth knowing before you promise someone a page:

- **Nothing submits or saves.** Inputs and filters change what you see, and that's all. A dashboard can't collect answers from readers or write anything back into a connected app.
- **It can't embed another website.** Links out are fine — they open in a new tab — but other sites can't be pulled into the page.

## Open your dashboard

When the page is ready, your agent posts a card in the chat showing a preview of the page, the filename, and the file size.

- Click **Open page** to open the dashboard in a new browser tab.
- Click the download button on the card to save the file to your computer.
- If something went wrong, the card shows **Retry** instead of a preview.

On the dashboard's own page, the header holds three actions:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Share** | Chooses who else can open the page. You only see it if you can manage the file. |
| **Full screen** | Opens the dashboard full screen. |
| **Download** | Saves the page to your computer. |

## Share your dashboard

Sharing a dashboard shares the page, not the agent behind it. People who open it see the result and nothing else: not your instructions, not your connected apps, not the chat it came from.

Click **Share** in the page header, then decide who gets in.

To invite named people, type into the people field and leave the role as **Can view**. View is the only role a dashboard has — nobody you invite can change the page. You can add a whole team or your organization from the same field.

Under **General access**, pick one:

| Setting | Who can open the page |
|---|---|
| **Only people invited** | Only the people, teams, and organizations you added. |
| **Anyone with the link** | Anyone who has the link. Download is switched off for them. |

Then copy the link and send it.

<Warning>
  There's no library of dashboards to browse. The link is how you get back to a page, so keep it — or ask your agent to build the dashboard again.
</Warning>
