title: "Connect, use, and disconnect apps"
description: "Connect external apps to Dataleap, approve access for agents, troubleshoot connection issues, and remove access."

Connected apps give your agents access to external tools and data. You connect an app once, then approve the specific tools an agent needs before it runs.

<Info>
  You need a Dataleap account, access to `https://app.dataleap.ai`, and an account or credentials for the app you want to connect. Your workspace admin may restrict some apps or tools.
</Info>

## Connect an app

You can connect an app while building an agent or from **Settings** > **Connected Apps**.

### Connect from settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connected Apps">
    Open `https://app.dataleap.ai`, open **Settings**, and select **Connected Apps**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose where to save the connection">
    Select **Personal** for a connection that belongs to you. If your workspace has teams, select a team to create a Team Connection. Team members may be able to use it from agents owned by that team, depending on your organization settings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the app">
    Click **Add Apps**, search for the app, and select it. Review its description, available tools, available triggers, and any credit notice before connecting.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start the connection">
    Click **Connect**. The setup screen depends on the app:

    - OAuth apps open an authorization window. Sign in to the provider, review the requested access, and approve it.
    - Some apps ask for credentials or other configuration in a Dataleap dialog. Enter the requested details and submit the form.
    - Apps that do not require authorization connect immediately.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Finish the provider or configuration screen and return to Dataleap. The app appears in **Connected Apps**. If the app is already connected, Dataleap may reuse the existing connection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Connect from an agent

When an agent needs an app that is not connected, describe the task in the agent's chat. Dataleap identifies the app it needs and shows a **Connect** action. Select it, complete the authorization or configuration screen, and return to the chat.

Connecting an app to your account does not automatically give every agent access to every tool. The agent asks for access separately when it needs to use the connection.

## Use a connected app

An app's available actions depend on the app, the connection type, your account's access, and your workspace's policies.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe what you want the agent to do">
    Ask the agent to use the connected app. For example: *"Find the open Jira issues assigned to me and summarize the blockers."*
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the requested tools">
    When the agent shows **Activate connections**, expand each app to review the tools it wants to add or remove. Tool names and descriptions appear in the list. Some tools may use additional credits.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Approve access">
    Click **Approve All** to activate the requested connections and let the agent continue. The approval applies to that agent's workflow. Dataleap asks again if the agent later needs a different set of tools.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Personal and team connections

- A **Personal** connection runs with the account you authorized and is intended for your agents.
- A Team Connection is saved for a team. Its availability depends on the team's use scope and organization settings.
- If the same app appears more than once, check the account and team labels before approving access. They may refer to different accounts or credentials.

## Which Slack connection to use

Slack is the one app you can connect twice: once with your personal Slack account, and once org-wide on behalf of your whole organization. Both can show up under **Connected Apps**, and they have different capabilities.

| | Personal connection | Org-wide connection |
|---|---|---|
| Default for | your personal agents | agents in a team space |
| Runs as | you | the organization's Slack bot |
| Best for | searching messages, reading and writing private channels and DMs, acting as you | most day-to-day Slack work: sending and reading messages, managing channels, reacting |
| Can't do | — | search messages, or read private channels and DMs the bot hasn't been added to |

If your agent can't find or read a private channel or DM, it's probably using the org-wide connection. Ask it in the chat:

> *"Please switch from the org-wide Slack connection to my personal Slack connection."*

<Note>
  Your admin may have installed Slack without user permissions — in that case personal connections aren't available, and the org-wide connection is your only option.
</Note>

For more detail about workspace-wide app and tool governance, see [Apps & permissions](/admin/connected-apps-management).

## Disconnect and deauthorize an app

Disconnect an app when you no longer want Dataleap to use the connection. If the app is used by agents, review the affected agents before confirming.

<Warning>
  Disconnecting removes the saved connection from Dataleap and removes that connection from the agents that use it. It does not delete the underlying records or data in the external app.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the connection list">
    Open **Settings** > **Connected Apps** and find the app. Use **Search** if needed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the disconnect dialog">
    Open the app's actions menu and select **Disconnect**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the impact">
    Review the account label, team information, and the list of agents that use the connection. Team Connections may be unavailable to other team members after you disconnect them.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose what happens to affected agents">
    **Deactivate affected agents** is selected by default. Leave it selected unless you have already replaced the connection and are ready to update the affected agents.

    Clear the option only if you intentionally want the agents to remain enabled while you replace or reconfigure their connection. An enabled agent without a usable connection may fail when it runs or may wait for a new connection.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the disconnect">
    Click **Disconnect App**. Dataleap removes the connection, removes its access from affected agents, and notifies those agents. If deactivation was selected, affected agents are also disabled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For supported managed integrations, Dataleap also removes the managed provider connection. Some providers keep a separate authorization record. If the provider still lists Dataleap under **Authorized apps**, **Connected applications**, or a similar setting, remove Dataleap there to complete provider-side deauthorization.

Disconnecting a Team Connection affects every team member or team agent that depends on that connection. Coordinate with the team before removing it.

## Troubleshoot connection issues

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| The app does not appear in the list | Clear the search, check whether you are browsing **Personal** or a team, and confirm that your workspace admin has not restricted the app. |
| The authorization window does not open | Allow popups for `https://app.dataleap.ai`, then select **Connect** again. |
| You closed the authorization window | Start the connection again and complete the provider flow before closing the window. |
| Dataleap says the credentials are invalid | Sign in to the intended provider account and verify the requested credentials or configuration. Then retry the connection. |
| The app connected but is not listed | Reopen **Settings** > **Connected Apps** and refresh the page. If it is still missing, try connecting again. |
| A requested tool is unavailable | Expand the connection request to see which tools are unavailable. Your workspace admin may have blocked them, or the provider account may not grant the required access. |
| An agent cannot use a connection after it was disconnected | Connect or authorize the app again, approve the requested tools in the agent, and re-enable the agent if it was deactivated. |
| A team connection or team option is missing | Confirm that you belong to the team and that your organization allows Team Connections. Contact your workspace admin if you need access. |
| The connection has expired or access was revoked | Follow the agent's **Reconnect** prompt and complete authorization again. If the provider still shows Dataleap as authorized, check the provider's authorized-app settings. |

If these steps do not resolve the issue, [contact Dataleap](https://www.dataleap.ai/contact-us) and include the app name, the step that failed, and the exact error message. Do not include passwords, access tokens, or other secrets.
